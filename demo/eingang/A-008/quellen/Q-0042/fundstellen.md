## F-0042-01
stelle: Abschnitt B.1 (Processing of Customer Data)
version: V-2026-09-12
zitat: >
  With respect to Customer Personal Data, Customer is the controller and Anthropic is Customer's processor. Each party will comply with its respective obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws in connection with the Services and the Customer Personal Data.
